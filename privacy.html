Wycliffe Legal Intelligence

Privacy notice

Wycliffe Legal Intelligence provides legal research and publishing tools. Visitors must create a free account or sign in to use the website’s research tools.

Account information

When you register by email, the account service receives your name and email. If you select Google sign-in, Google provides your basic profile and email for authentication. Supabase handles authentication and stores account records. The site administrator has access to verified registration details through the private administration dashboard. The site does not request access to your Google Drive, Gmail or contacts.

Research notes and local storage

Private notes are stored against your account. Browser storage maintains your session and local research preferences. Do not upload confidential client material or sensitive personal information.

Account control

You have the option to sign out and delete saved cloud notes through your workspace. Contact the administrator for questions or requests concerning your account information, including account deletion. Google account permissions are managed through your Google account.

External services

Google, Supabase, GitHub Pages and external legal sources have their own privacy practices. External research links take you to sites outside Wycliffe Legal Intelligence.
