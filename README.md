# Wycliffe Legal Intelligence

Open-source legal technology by Wycliffe Alpha Mponda, Tanzanian legal author and researcher.

## Interactive research studio

Open `index.html` in your browser. The studio has a statutory citation formatter, citation scanner, text comparison tool and a session-based authority register with CSV export. No backend or dependencies are needed. Only use synthetic or public material. Outputs are not verified legal advice.
