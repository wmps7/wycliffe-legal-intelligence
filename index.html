About Wycliffe Alpha Mponda

Wycliffe Alpha Mponda is a Tanzanian legal author, legal researcher and professional writer based in Dar es Salaam. His work focuses on Tanzanian commercial law, civil procedure, legal research, document preparation and legal publishing.

He co-authored Commercial Law in Tanzania: Law, Procedure, Documents and Practice with Laus Abdallah Rashidi (first edition, 2026). He also writes on civil procedure and develops open-source legal publishing and research tools through Wycliffe Legal Intelligence.
