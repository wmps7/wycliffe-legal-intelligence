Case treatment and citation research

Cited-by research

Find later judgments mentioning an authority

Enter the exact party name or neutral citation. Search TanzLII for references in other judgments, then read each result to determine whether the original case was followed, distinguished, criticised or overruled.

Reference quality

Case verification method

  1. Confirm the court, parties, case number and decision date.
  2. Read the judgment itself instead of relying on the search snippet.
  3. Identify the issue decided, reasoning and order.
  4. Search for later references to the case and compare their treatment.
  5. Record the pinpoint page or paragraph and source URL.

          Legal practice workflows
          Practical starting points for law firms, legal researchers and authors. These workflows identify questions and evidence requirements, not legal deadlines or automatic legal conclusions.
          Litigation research
          1. Identify court, jurisdiction and proceeding.
          2. Find the governing rule and latest amendments.
          3. Read the original judgment and record its reasoning.
          4. Check the court's directions and confirm filing deadlines.
          Judiciary portal
          Corporate compliance
          1. Record company and registration details.
          2. Identify applicable company law and filing obligations.
          3. Verify current requirements with the responsible registry.
          4. Retain receipts and proof of compliance.
          Business Registrations and Licensing Agency (BRELA)
          Contract preparation
          1. Identify parties, authority and transaction purpose.
          2. Record terms, payment, obligations and risk allocation.
          3. Compare only authorised versions.
          4. Obtain qualified legal review before execution.
          Compare document text
          Legal publishing
          1. Prepare chapter structure and citation register.
          2. Confirm each authority against the primary source.
          3. Audit numbering and footnotes.
          4. Proofread PDF, EPUB and web editions.
